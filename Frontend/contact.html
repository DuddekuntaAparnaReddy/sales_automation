What is Salesbot and how does it help users?

Salesbot is an AI-powered sales and marketing automation platform that provides instant customer support, smart recommendations, and automated assistance through AI agents.

How can I interact with the AI assistant?

Users can interact with Salesbot through AI chat or voice assistance to ask questions, get suggestions, and receive real-time responses.

Can Salesbot recommend products based on my requirements?

Yes. Salesbot understands user needs such as budget, preferences, and purpose to provide personalized product or service recommendations.

Can I directly talk with the AI assistant?

Yes. Salesbot supports a browser-based AI voice assistant allowing users to communicate directly with the AI assistant using voice in the web app. (AI-powered telephony integration is planned as a future scope feature.)

How will I receive updates about offers and events?

Salesbot provides automated promotional campaigns and event invitations to keep users informed about relevant offers, updates, and activities.
